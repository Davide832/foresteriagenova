The apartment

A 50 sqm apartment on the 17th floor of the Torre Elah, in Pegli, fully furnished, with views over the sea and the city. Living room with sofa bed and access to the covered terrace, fitted kitchen with induction hob and dishwasher, double bedroom with walk-in wardrobe, bathroom with shower cubicle; 3 beds in all. Independent air conditioning, laundry room with washing machine, fibre connection with Wi-Fi, smart TV, electric roller shutters. Covered garage in the complex, connected to the apartment by lift; concierge, CCTV, badge-controlled access, roof garden and supermarket in the complex. Suited to an executive, a consultant or a professional couple, for stays of 1 to 12 months. On request, housekeeping option: weekly cleaning and change of linen. Non-smoking apartment; pets are not allowed. Electricity, gas, water and the waste tax (TARI) are not included in the rent and are paid by the company.

The area and connections

In Pegli, in western Genoa, 100 metres from the beach and the seafront promenade, with shops and services within walking distance. Genova Pegli railway station is a few minutes' walk away and connects Sestri Ponente and the city centre in a quarter of an hour; the Genova Pegli motorway exit and Cristoforo Colombo airport are a few minutes away by car.

Close to the main work hubs

  • Porto Petroli and the Multedo terminals about 5 minutes by car
  • Fincantieri and the Sestri Ponente shipyards about 10 minutes away
  • Cristoforo Colombo airport and the PSA terminal at Pra' about 10 minutes away
  • Erzelli technology park about 15 minutes away
  • Port of Genoa, cruise terminal and Ansaldo Energia in about 20 minutes by car
