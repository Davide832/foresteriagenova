The area and connections

In Cogoleto, on the western Riviera between Arenzano and Varazze, halfway between Genoa and Savona. Cogoleto railway station, on the Genoa–Savona line, is within walking distance; the Arenzano exit of the A10 motorway is about 10 minutes away. Beach, shops and restaurants in the town centre.

Close to the main work hubs

  • Port and platform of Vado Ligure, shipyards and plants of Savona about 25 minutes by car
  • Fincantieri Sestri Ponente and Genoa airport about 25-30 minutes by car
  • Erzelli technology park about 30 minutes
  • Cogoleto station a few minutes' walk away: Genoa Principe in 35-40 minutes by train
