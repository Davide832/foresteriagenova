The apartment

Newly renovated, fully furnished 102 sqm apartment on the second floor of a handsome historic building with lift. 5 rooms: hallway, eat-in kitchen, 3 twin bedrooms with single beds, bathroom with shower. Every room has a Smart TV; WiFi, washing machine, dishwasher, air conditioning and independent heating complete the equipment. Ideal for teams of up to 6 people on assignment in Genoa.

The area and connections

In the heart of Sampierdarena, a few minutes from Genoa Sampierdarena railway station and the port gates: a strategic position for staff working in the port basin, in ship repair and in the companies of western Genoa. The Genova Ovest motorway exit is about 5 minutes away by car; shops, supermarkets and restaurants are right downstairs along Via Sampierdarena. Free parking is easy to find in the area, a real advantage for staff travelling by car.

Close to the main work hubs

  • Port of Genoa, terminals and ship-repair yards a few minutes away
  • Hitachi Rail in the district and Ansaldo Energia (Campi area) about 10 minutes away
  • Fincantieri Sestri Ponente, ABB (Via Albareto) and the Erzelli technology park 10-15 minutes by car
  • Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT, Via Morego) about 15 minutes by car
