The area and connections

In Sestri Ponente, the heart of Genoa's shipbuilding district: an ideal position for staff working in the shipyards and plants of western Genoa. Genoa Cristoforo Colombo airport is a few minutes away by car, Genoa Sestri Ponente railway station is within walking distance and the nearest motorway exit is Genova Aeroporto. The street is well served by shops and restaurants.

Close to the main work hubs

  • Fincantieri and the Sestri Ponente shipyards a few minutes away
  • ABB (Via Albareto) and Cristoforo Colombo airport about 5 minutes by car
  • Erzelli technology park (Ericsson, Esaote, Siemens, IIT) about 10 minutes away
  • Ansaldo Energia and the Port of Genoa about 10-15 minutes by car
